Objectives

Background / Scenario

As a computer ages, you may wish to perform a hardware upgrade to extend its useful lifespan and improve its performance.

You have decided to update a computer that was purchased about a year ago. You have become an avid gamer who required a more powerful and larger monitor that allows for a more immersive gaming experience. You have also ventured into virtual reality games. The new monitor requires a more up-to-date graphics card to support all the new features, such as higher resolution, faster refresh rate, and lower response time.

In this activity, you will gather information about hardware components to update your computer.

Reflection
What are the considerations when deciding to upgrade components vs replacing the computer? List the advantages and disadvantages of the decision.
Answer Area
Upgrading a computer may be advantageous when replacing a few components increases performance significantly. For example, upgrading an HDD with an SSD can speed up the startup of operating systems and applications without replacing other components. On the other hand, it would be advantageous to purchase a new computer instead of upgrading most major components. For example, when upgrading a processor, it may be necessary to upgrade the motherboard, RAM, video graphics card, and other components. At this point, it becomes more advantageous to replace the computer with a finished product or a do-it-yourself build when a new computer purchase is more compatible with the current software and manufacturers’ warranty.
